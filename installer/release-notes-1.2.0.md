Simpler to use.

- Open photos on their own: no folder needed. They go straight into the editor and stay under Recent photos in the sidebar. Folders still work too.
- Click a photo to edit it. To pick several for batch edits, tick the circle on a photo (or Cmd/Ctrl/Shift-click); Select all and Done sit in the bar.
- The Edit tab always works: it opens the last photo you edited, or the first one.
- Drop photos on the window to open just those photos; drop a folder to open the folder.

The apps aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On a Mac, right-click Lightshaper and choose Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
