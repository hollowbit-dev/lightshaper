Lightshaper opens many more kinds of photos.

- New formats: TIFF (8 and 16-bit), HEIC and HEIF on Windows too, JPEG XL, Photoshop PSD (the flattened image), and more RAW files (GoPro GPR, Kodak, Hasselblad, Phase One, Leaf, Sinar and others LibRaw reads).
- Export to TIFF, 8 or 16-bit, losslessly compressed.
- Open photos directly, not just folders. On a Mac the Open window takes photos or a folder; on Windows there's Open photos and Open a folder. Picking one photo opens it straight in the editor.
- Transparent PNG and PSD areas are shown over white.

The apps aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On a Mac, right-click Lightshaper and choose Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
