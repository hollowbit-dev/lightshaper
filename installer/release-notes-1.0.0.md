The first release of Lightshaper: a free, offline photo editor for Mac and Windows.

- Open a folder of photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC (Mac) and camera RAW from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax, Leica, iPhone ProRAW and more.
- Light, colour, tone curve, colour mixer, colour grading, effects, sharpening and noise reduction.
- Gradient and radial masks, a white balance picker, crop and straighten, before / after.
- 14 looks, your own looks, and Match a photo.
- Opens Lightroom presets (.xmp and .lrtemplate). Lightshaper is not affiliated with Adobe.
- Star ratings, copy and paste edits, batch export to JPEG, PNG or WebP with camera info (never your location).
- Your originals are never changed. No account, no ads, no tracking, no AI.

The apps aren't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet. On a Mac, right-click Lightshaper and choose Open the first time. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway.
